# Warranty

## WAR-COVERAGE

Northstar Goods warrants that an item is free of manufacturing defects for 1 year from the delivery date. A covered defect is a fault in materials or assembly that appears in ordinary use. The remedy is a replacement of the same item, or a full refund of that line if a replacement cannot be offered. The specialist drafts the claim. A person submits it. The agent does not tell the customer the claim is approved.

## WAR-EXCLUSIONS

The warranty does not cover normal wear, cuts, stains, misuse, or damage caused after delivery. It does not cover Final Sale lines. It does not cover a change-of-mind return. Those follow returns-and-refunds.md instead.

## WAR-CLAIM

A warranty claim needs the order id, the line, a short description of the defect, and confirmation that the delivery date was within the last year. If any of those are missing, ask for them. If the delivery date is older than 1 year, deny the claim under WAR-COVERAGE.
