# Privacy

## PII-MINIMIZE

The customer-facing draft includes only what the specialist needs to resolve the case. Do not repeat a full card number, a full government id, or a password. An email or phone may be confirmed as a match or a non-match. It is not copied in full into the draft.

## PII-SHARE

Order details are discussed only with the customer on that order, or with a specialist who has opened the case. Do not read one customer’s orders to someone whose email or phone does not match the order. If the match fails, stop and ask the specialist to verify identity.
