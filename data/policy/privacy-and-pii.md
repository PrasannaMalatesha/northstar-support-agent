# Privacy

What support may say about a customer. Payment numbers are never collected in the conversation.

## PII-MINIMIZE

**Rule**: The customer-facing draft includes only what the specialist needs to resolve the case. Do not repeat a full card number, a full government id, or a password. An email or phone may be confirmed as a match or a non-match. It is not copied in full into the draft.

**Applies when**: The customer's message contains a contact detail, a card number, or a password.

**Agent must not**: Copy a full card number, a full government id, or a password into the draft.

**Example**: The customer pastes a card number and asks for a refund. The draft talks about the order and does not repeat the number. It cites PII-MINIMIZE.

## PII-SHARE

**Rule**: Order details are discussed only with the customer on that order, through a specialist who has bound the case to that customer. Do not describe one customer's order to a case that is bound to someone else. If the case has no customer, do not read an order. Say that a customer must be picked first.

**Applies when**: Someone asks for order details and the case customer may not be the buyer.

**Agent must not**: Read another customer's order aloud, or treat an unbound case as identified.

**Example**: The case is bound to Avery and the order id belongs to Blake. The draft says the order was not found for this customer and gives no line details. It cites PII-SHARE.

## PII-PAYMENT

**Rule**: Support never asks for a full card number, a security code, or a card expiry date. The payment method on the order, when the order record stores it, is named only as the brand and the last four digits already on that record.

**Applies when**: A refund, a duplicate charge, or a gift card needs a payment fact.

**Agent must not**: Ask the customer to type a full card number or a security code into the case.

**Example**: The customer offers to send the full card number. The draft says not to send it, citing PII-PAYMENT.

## PII-DELETE

**Rule**: A request to delete customer data goes to the privacy team. The privacy team responds within 45 days. The agent does not delete the customer, the orders, or the case from the console.

**Applies when**: The customer asks Northstar to delete their information.

**Agent must not**: Promise that data is already deleted, or delete the record from the case desk.

**Example**: The customer asks for their account to be erased. The draft says the privacy team responds within 45 days and the case escalates. It cites PII-DELETE.
