# Promotions and gift cards

Codes, gift cards, and store credit. The free-shipping threshold is the one in SHIP-OPTIONS. This file does not restate that amount.

## PROMO-CODES

**Rule**: An order accepts one promotion code. A code cannot be added after the order is placed. A code that did not apply at purchase is not applied later by support.

**Applies when**: A customer asks to add, stack, or replace a code.

**Agent must not**: Add a code to a placed order, or stack a second code.

**Example**: The order is placed with no code. The customer found a code the next day. The draft says a code cannot be added after the order is placed, citing PROMO-CODES.

## PROMO-RETURNS

**Rule**: A refund is the amount actually paid for the line after discounts. When a bundle discount covered several lines, the discount is split across those lines in proportion to each line's price before the discount. Support does not refund the pre-discount price.

**Applies when**: A discounted line or a bundle is being returned.

**Agent must not**: Refund the shelf price when the customer paid less.

**Example**: A line's shelf price was higher and the customer paid less after a code. The refund uses the amount paid. The draft cites PROMO-RETURNS.

## PROMO-THRESHOLD

**Rule**: If a return leaves the remaining merchandise below the free-shipping threshold in SHIP-OPTIONS, Northstar does not charge that shipping back. Shipping already paid is refunded only when a shipping rule says so.

**Applies when**: A return might drop the order under the free-shipping threshold.

**Agent must not**: Subtract the original shipping charge from the refund only because the order is now under the threshold.

**Example**: An order qualified for free standard shipping. One line is returned and the rest is under the threshold. The line refund does not grow a new shipping charge. The draft cites PROMO-THRESHOLD.

## GC-TERMS

**Rule**: A Northstar gift card has no expiry and no fee. It is not redeemable for cash, except where the law requires cash redemption. A gift card is final sale under REF-FINAL-SALE.

**Applies when**: A customer asks how a gift card works, or asks to cash it out.

**Agent must not**: Promise cash for a gift card, or an expiry date.

**Example**: A customer asks to cash a $25 gift card. The draft says it does not expire, has no fee, and is not cashed out, citing GC-TERMS.

## GC-LOST

**Rule**: A lost gift card is replaced only when it was registered to the customer record. An unregistered gift card that is lost is not replaced. Support does not ask for the full card number. The customer identifies the card by the account it was registered to.

**Applies when**: A customer says a gift card was lost or stolen.

**Agent must not**: Replace an unregistered card, or ask the customer to send the full card number.

**Example**: The card is on the customer record. The proposal is a replacement card. The draft cites GC-LOST. A card that was never registered is not replaced.

## STORE-CREDIT

**Rule**: Store credit has no expiry and is not transferable to another customer. It is spent on a later Northstar order. It is not cash and not a gift card. Partial credits under REF-PARTIAL and gift returns under REF-GIFT are store credit.

**Applies when**: A customer asks what store credit is, or asks to give it to someone else.

**Agent must not**: Move store credit to another person, or call it a cash refund.

**Example**: Store credit from a used-item return stays on that customer. The draft cites STORE-CREDIT.
