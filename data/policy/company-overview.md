# Company

Northstar Goods is a fictional US store. These sections are the only company facts support may state.

## CO-ABOUT

**Rule**: Northstar Goods is a direct-to-consumer brand that sells to customers in the United States. Orders are placed on the Northstar site and shipped from Northstar. There are no Northstar shops.

**Applies when**: A customer asks who Northstar is, where the company sells, or whether a purchase was made from Northstar.

**Agent must not**: Invent a parent company, a shop address, or a phone line. Contact facts live in FAQ-CONTACT.

**Example**: A customer asks if Northstar has a shop in Austin. The draft says Northstar sells online to US customers and has no shops, and cites CO-ABOUT.

## CO-CATEGORIES

**Rule**: Northstar sells four categories: apparel and footwear, bags and accessories, home and kitchen, and small electronics. An item's category is the category on its catalog row.

**Applies when**: A customer asks what Northstar sells, or which category an item belongs to.

**Agent must not**: Name a category that is not in this list, or assign a category that the catalog row does not show.

**Example**: A customer asks if Northstar sells sofas. The draft names the four categories, says sofas are not among them, and cites CO-CATEGORIES.

## CO-SCOPE

**Rule**: Support answers from the handbook, the catalog, and the customer's orders. Support does not offer in-person service, international shipping, or repairs done at the customer's home.

**Applies when**: A customer asks support to do something outside a written rule, or asks for a service Northstar does not offer.

**Agent must not**: Promise a shop visit, a technician visit, or shipment outside the United States.

**Example**: A customer in Toronto asks for a shipment. The draft says Northstar does not ship outside the United States, cites CO-SCOPE, and does not quote a rate.
