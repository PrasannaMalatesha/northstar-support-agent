# General questions

Hours, contact, and account. Sizes come from the catalog, not from advice.

## FAQ-HOURS

**Rule**: Specialists answer messages on weekdays from 9:00 to 18:00 US Central Time. Messages received outside those hours are answered on the next weekday. The agent may still draft a reply outside those hours. It does not promise a human callback at night or on a weekend.

**Applies when**: A customer asks when a person will reply.

**Agent must not**: Promise a night or weekend callback.

**Example**: A message arrives Saturday. The draft says a specialist answers on the next weekday, citing FAQ-HOURS.

## FAQ-CONTACT

**Rule**: Customers reach Northstar at help@northstar.example. There is no phone line and no published street address for support.

**Applies when**: A customer asks how to contact Northstar.

**Agent must not**: Invent a phone number, a street address, or a second email.

**Example**: The customer asks for a phone number. The draft gives help@northstar.example and says there is no phone line, citing FAQ-CONTACT.

## FAQ-ACCOUNT

**Rule**: A customer is identified by the email and phone stored on their customer record. An order belongs to the customer id on that order. The specialist binds the case. The agent does not choose the customer.

**Applies when**: The specialist is about to talk about an order, or the customer asks which account an order sits on.

**Agent must not**: Bind a customer the specialist did not look up, or create a customer from the conversation.

**Example**: Two emails are in the message. The draft asks the specialist to look up the customer. It does not pick one. It cites FAQ-ACCOUNT.

## FAQ-PASSWORD

**Rule**: Password resets use the link on the Northstar sign-in page. Support does not set a password, does not ask the customer to send a password, and does not read a password back.

**Applies when**: A customer cannot sign in to the store.

**Agent must not**: Ask for the current password, or invent a temporary password.

**Example**: The customer asks support to reset the password in the case. The draft points them to the sign-in link and cites FAQ-PASSWORD.

## FAQ-EMAILS

**Rule**: Marketing email is stopped from the unsubscribe link in the message footer. A support case does not by itself stop marketing email. Order and refund mail still sends.

**Applies when**: A customer asks to stop promotional email.

**Agent must not**: Promise that opening a case unsubscribes the customer.

**Example**: The customer wants no more promotions. The draft tells them to use the unsubscribe link in the footer, citing FAQ-EMAILS.

## FAQ-SIZING

**Rule**: A size or variant question is answered only from the catalog row for that item. If the row has no sizes, or the customer asks whether an item will fit, the draft abstains. Fit advice is not in the handbook.

**Applies when**: A customer asks what sizes exist, or whether an item will fit them.

**Agent must not**: Recommend a size from general knowledge, or describe a fit the catalog row does not state.

**Example**: The customer asks if the coat runs small. The draft abstains and says fit is not in the catalog row, citing FAQ-SIZING.
