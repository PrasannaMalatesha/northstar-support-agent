# General questions

## FAQ-HOURS

Specialists answer messages on weekdays from 9:00 to 18:00 US Central Time. Messages received outside those hours are answered on the next weekday. The agent may still draft a reply outside those hours. It does not promise a human callback at night.

## FAQ-CONTACT

Customers reach Northstar at help@northstar.example. There is no phone line. Do not invent a phone number or a street address.

## FAQ-ACCOUNT

A customer is identified by the email and phone stored on their customer record. An order belongs to the customer id on that order. Password resets are not handled in this console. Tell the customer to use the link on the sign-in page, and do not ask them to send a password.
